Hello, I'm Merlyn 👋

I'm a passionate music teacher based in West Byfleet, Surrey, with ten years of experience teaching piano, singing, and music theory to children aged 5–16 in school environments. I hold a Grade 8 in Piano, Grade 6 in Music Theory (Distinction) and Grade 6 in Singing (Merit) — all certified by Trinity College London.

My journey spans from leading choirs and managing school events in India to now offering personalised private piano lessons here in the UK. I believe every child has musical potential — it just takes the right approach to unlock it.

Training Curriculum
